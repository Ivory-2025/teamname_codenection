1. Project Overview
1.1 The Problem
Planning a trip is stressful because travelers must juggle disconnected apps for booking, budgeting, and scheduling. This creates several major pain points:
Coordination Chaos: It is extremely difficult to align multiple schedules, budgets, and opinions for group trips.
Rigid Itineraries: Plans are usually recorded on static documents that cannot adapt to unexpected real-world delays.
Uncentralized travel platform: Travelers are forced to use a messy combination of tools like Wanderlog for maps, TripIt for flights, Splitwise for money, and WhatsApp for communication.
As a result, travelers waste time and easily panic when plans change, while businesses lose potential revenue when overwhelmed users abandon the planning process entirely. Existing solutions fall short because they only focus on specific niches.
1.2 Target Audience
Our primary audience consists of busy youth and young adults who frequently organize group vacations.
Their core frustrations: The immense time wasted juggling disjointed apps to align schedules and shared expenses.
Their main stressor: Dealing with rigid, static itineraries that fail to adapt to unexpected real-world disruptions like flight delays or weather changes.
1.3 Our Solution: TripManager
TripManager is an end-to-end, collaborative travel platform designed to make planning centralized and dynamic.
Features:
Frictionless Collaboration: Friends can instantly join a trip board via a link/QR code to align schedules and opinions.
Smart Planning: Users input exact budgets and select "vibe tags" to generate affordable, customized travel plans with interactive route maps.
All in One Booking: Easy in-app booking for flights, hotels, and tickets.
Smart Fixes: An adaptive feature that instantly recalibrates the itinerary if unexpected disruptions occur.
Smart Expense Tracking: Instantly scans receipts and auto-calculates everyone's exact share. 
Social Community Feed: Users can discover, like, and clone successful trip blueprints with a single click.
TripManager doesn't just organize trips, it actively enhances group safety, fairness, and engagement, creating a highly scalable product that modern travelers actually want to use.
